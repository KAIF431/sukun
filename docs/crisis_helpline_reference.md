### 2. International & Overseas Users

Agar koi user Bharat se bahar hai, to unke liye global confidential support portals:

* **Find A Helpline (Global Directory):** [`https://findahelpline.com/`](https://findahelpline.com/) (130+ mulkon ki free crisis lines)
* **Befrienders Worldwide:** [`https://www.befrienders.org/`](https://www.befrienders.org/) (Volunteer-led emotional support)
* **Local Emergency:** Apne muqami police/ambulance number (jaise US/Canada: `911` ya `988`, UK: `999` ya `111`, Gulf: `999`).
